(Services · Automation)

Automation thatruns your busywork

Your team should spend its time on clients, not copy-pasting between apps. We find the repetitive work in your business and hand it to reliable automations and AI assistants.

(01) Start with the work, not the tools

We begin by mapping how work actually moves through your business: where leads arrive, who touches them, what gets retyped, and where things slip. Only then do we choose tools. Often the biggest wins are small: a form that files itself into your CRM, a quote that sends itself, a report that builds itself every Monday.

(02) AI assistants that know your business

An AI assistant can answer common enquiries on your website, WhatsApp or Instagram, qualify leads and book calls into your calendar at any hour. We train assistants on your own services, prices and policies, keep a human in the loop for anything sensitive, and make sure every conversation lands in your CRM.

(03) Reliable, documented, yours

Automations fail quietly if nobody watches them. Every workflow we build includes error alerts, a plain-English document explaining what it does, and access that stays in your accounts. You are never locked into us to keep your business running.

(04) Tools we work with

Make, Zapier, n8n, HubSpot, Pipedrive, Notion, Airtable, Google Workspace, Slack, WhatsApp Business, Stripe, Shopify and the OpenAI and Anthropic APIs, plus custom code when an off-the-shelf connector is not enough.

(05) What you get

  • Workflow audit and roadmap
  • Lead capture and routing
  • CRM setup and sync
  • AI chat and booking assistants
  • Invoicing and reporting automation
  • Monitoring, alerts and documentation

(FAQ) Common questions

Do we need to change the software we use?

Usually not. We connect the tools you already have and only suggest changes where they save real time.

Is AI safe to put in front of customers?

With the right limits, yes. Assistants only answer from approved information and hand anything unusual to a person.

What happens if an automation breaks?

You get an alert immediately, and every workflow is documented so it can be fixed quickly by us or your own team.
