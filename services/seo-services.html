SEO that bringsenquiries, not just traffic

Ranking only matters if it brings people who want to buy. We fix what stops Google from understanding your site, build the pages your customers are actually searching for, and track results in enquiries, not vanity numbers.

(01) Technical SEO first

Slow pages, broken links, missing titles and pages Google cannot crawl quietly hold a site back. We audit and fix these first: speed, indexing, structured data, sitemaps and mobile usability.

(02) Local SEO

For searches like "near me" or a service plus your city, Google leans heavily on your Business Profile, reviews and local pages. We set up and optimise your Google Business Profile, build accurate city and service pages, and help you earn genuine reviews.

(03) Content that answers real searches

We research what your customers type into Google, then write pages and guides that answer it better than anyone else ranking. Every page is written for people first and structured so search engines understand it.

(04) Links and reputation

Google trusts sites that other trusted sites mention. We earn listings on relevant directories, industry sites and publications, never through bought or spammy links that put your site at risk.

(05) Reporting you can act on

Each month you see rankings, clicks and, most importantly, enquiries from search, straight from Google Search Console and your analytics, with what we will do next.

(FAQ) Common questions

How long does SEO take?

Technical fixes can show results within weeks. Ranking for competitive terms usually takes three to six months of steady work.

Do you guarantee first position on Google?

No honest agency can. We commit to the work and report transparently on rankings, traffic and enquiries.

Can you do SEO for a website you did not build?

Yes. We start with an audit of your current site and work with whichever platform it runs on.
