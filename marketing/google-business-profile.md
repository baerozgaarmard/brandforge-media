2. **Business category:** primary `Website designer`. Add as additional categories: `Internet marketing service`, `Marketing agency`, `Graphic designer`, `Search engine optimisation agency`, `Social media agency` (pick whichever of these Google offers).

## Other fields

- **Opening hours:** your real working hours (e.g. Mon–Sat, 10:00–19:00).
- **Opening date:** when BrandForge started.
- **Logo:** your round BrandForge badge (`public/brand/logo-badge.webp` in the project).
- **Cover photo:** the site's share image (`public/og.png`).
- **Photos:** add 5–10: screenshots of your concept projects (`public/shots/*.webp`), the 3D forge, and later real client work. Profiles with photos get noticeably more clicks.
