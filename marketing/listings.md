### Long description (~150 words)
BrandForge is an independent digital studio for brands that would rather be remembered than just seen.

We build immersive 3D websites with WebGL and Three.js that stay fast on everyday phones and laptops. We design custom Shopify and Shopify Plus stores from a blank page — no bought templates — with 3D product views and conversion-focused journeys. We automate the repetitive parts of running a business, from lead routing and CRM sync to AI assistants that answer and book enquiries around the clock. And we plan, shoot and edit short-form social content with the same craft we put into our websites.

Design, development, 3D and strategy sit under one roof, so nothing gets lost in translation between agencies. Every project starts with a working prototype within the first two weeks.
