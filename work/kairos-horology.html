(01) The brief

A watchmaker releasing 150 hand-finished pieces a year at ₹8 lakh each. Collectors buy on detail, and a flat product photo shows none of the 200 hours inside each watch.

(02) Our approach

A Shopify storefront built around a macro 3D movement viewer: collectors can open the caseback and watch the balance wheel beat, with allocation-based drops for each release.
