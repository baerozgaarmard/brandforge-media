Concept · 2026

(01) The brief

A watchmaker releasing 150 hand-finished pieces a year at ₹8 lakh each. Collectors buy on detail, and a flat product photo shows none of the 200 hours inside each watch.

(02) Our approach

A Shopify storefront built around a macro 3D movement viewer: collectors can open the caseback and watch the balance wheel beat, with allocation-based drops for each release.

(03) The challenge in detail

Collectors buy fine watches on detail: the finishing of the movement, the bevels, the way the balance wheel moves. Photographs flatten all of it. With only 150 pieces a year, the brand also needed a fair way to allocate each release without a website crash or a queue of disappointed collectors.

(04) Design direction

Warm blacks, brass accents and editorial typography, like a fine watch catalogue. Product pages lead with craft: the hours of hand-finishing, the materials and the maker, before the price.

(05) How it works

A Shopify storefront with a macro 3D viewer: collectors can open the caseback and watch the movement beat, built from Blender models optimised for the web. Releases use an allocation system: collectors register interest, the brand reviews requests, and invited buyers receive a private checkout link.

(06) What this concept shows

Limited, high-value products need more than a standard store: a way to show craft and a fair way to sell scarce stock. The approach fits jewellery, art editions and handmade luxury goods.

(07) Deliverables

  • Shopify storefront
  • 3D movement viewer
  • Allocation drop system
  • Macro content series
