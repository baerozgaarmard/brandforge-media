(01) The brief

A private developer selling eight ₹100 crore+ residences before ground breaks. Buyers fly in from four continents, so the sale has to happen before anyone can walk the property.

(02) Our approach

We built a real-time 3D walkthrough of every residence, lit for golden hour and explorable room by room in the browser, then wired enquiries into a concierge pipeline that books private viewings automatically.

(03) The challenge in detail

Selling a home that does not exist yet is a trust problem. Buyers of residences at this level are used to private tours, not brochures, and most of them live abroad. They need to understand light, scale and view before they will even book a flight. A conventional property site with renders and a PDF would put Aurum in the same pile as every other off-plan launch.

(04) Design direction

We kept the interface almost silent: warm stone colours, generous space and serif headlines that feel like an architect's monograph. The 3D tours carry the emotion. Each residence is lit for golden hour, and the camera moves at walking pace so the space feels real rather than like a video game.

(05) How it works

Every residence loads as a compressed real-time model that runs smoothly on a laptop and falls back to a guided video on older phones. Buyers can move room by room, switch between day and evening light, and save favourite views. Each enquiry is scored on budget, timeline and residence interest, then passed to the right sales concierge with a private-viewing slot already proposed.

(06) What this concept shows

Real-time 3D can replace the first site visit for premium property, and automation can make sure every serious enquiry gets a personal reply within minutes. The same approach suits villa projects, plotted developments and hospitality launches across India.

(07) Deliverables

  • Immersive WebGL property tours
  • Brand identity refresh
  • Lead scoring + CRM automation
  • Private-viewing booking flow
