(01) The brief

A Parisian perfume house moving from boutiques to global direct-to-consumer, at €240 a bottle. Fragrance is impossible to smell online, so the store had to sell the feeling instead.

(02) Our approach

A custom Shopify Plus flagship where each scent is a 3D bottle you can turn and uncork, paired with scent-note storytelling, gifting flows and a discovery-set funnel.

(03) Deliverables

  • Shopify Plus custom theme
  • 3D bottle configurator
  • Discovery-set funnel
  • Klaviyo lifecycle flows
