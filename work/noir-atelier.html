(01) The brief

A Parisian perfume house moving from boutiques to global direct-to-consumer, at €240 a bottle. Fragrance is impossible to smell online, so the store had to sell the feeling instead.

(02) Our approach

A custom Shopify Plus flagship where each scent is a 3D bottle you can turn and uncork, paired with scent-note storytelling, gifting flows and a discovery-set funnel.

(03) The challenge in detail

Fragrance is bought through the nose, which a website cannot reach. Noir Atelier's boutiques relied on staff guiding customers through notes and memories. Online, a bottle on a white background says nothing about the scent, and at this price customers need a reason to trust what they cannot smell.

(04) Design direction

Black lacquer, cream type and a single deep red: the store borrows the restraint of the boutique. Each fragrance gets an editorial page with its notes written as a short story, so customers choose a mood rather than a list of ingredients.

(05) How it works

A custom Shopify Plus theme built from a blank page, with a 3D bottle customers can turn and uncork. A discovery set of small samples is the entry product: once it ships, a Klaviyo sequence follows up with tasting notes and a credit towards a full bottle, turning a low-risk first order into a confident second one.

(06) What this concept shows

Premium products that are hard to judge online sell through storytelling and a low-risk first step. The same structure works for skincare, tea, coffee and other sensory products, including Indian D2C brands selling nationwide.

(07) Deliverables

  • Shopify Plus custom theme
  • 3D bottle configurator
  • Discovery-set funnel
  • Klaviyo lifecycle flows
