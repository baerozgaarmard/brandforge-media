(01) The brief

An electric grand-tourer revealed to the world in a single night, with a ₹1 lakh reservation fee and a waitlist that had to convert while the livestream was still running.

(02) Our approach

A cinematic launch site with a real-time car configurator: paint, wheels and interior rendered live, a countdown-to-reveal mode, and one-tap reservations built for traffic spikes.

(03) The challenge in detail

A car reveal happens once. The site has to hold up while a livestream sends a wave of visitors at the same moment, explain an unfamiliar electric car in seconds, and take a paid reservation before the excitement fades. A slow page or a broken checkout on launch night cannot be fixed later.

(04) Design direction

Dark, cinematic and fast: the car is the hero, lit like a studio shoot, with a countdown that turns into the configurator the moment the reveal happens. Copy is short and confident, with the key numbers (range, acceleration and price) always one glance away.

(05) How it works

The configurator renders paint, wheels and interior in real time in the browser. Static pages and assets are served from the edge so traffic spikes do not slow the site, and the reservation flow takes a deposit in two steps with a confirmation that is ready to share on social media.

(06) What this concept shows

Launches need speed, spectacle and a frictionless way to commit. The same pattern fits product drops, real-estate launches and event ticketing, wherever demand arrives all at once.

(07) Deliverables

  • WebGL car configurator
  • Launch-night microsite
  • Reservation + deposit flow
  • Social launch films
